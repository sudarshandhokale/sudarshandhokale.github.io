CASE 01 / 04

When customer data cannot wait for the next sync.

THE PROBLEM

Periodic warehouse synchronization left audience information stale, affecting campaign targeting.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Led architecture and hands-on delivery of Kafka-based customer data synchronization with Braze, replacing the periodic synchronization.

WHY IT MATTERED

Reduced data latency and addressed stale audience information so targeting could use more current customer data.

EXPLORE THE CHANGE

BEFORE · Initial situation

◎
WarehouseStored customer attributes
↯
Periodic synchronizationUpdates wait for the next run
◈
Braze audiencesTargeting inputs can become stale

Audience freshness depended on periodic warehouse synchronization.

AFTER · Implemented approach

◎
Customer eventsChanges become events
↯
Kafka-based synchronizationEvent-driven delivery
◈
Braze audiencesMore current customer attributes

Event-driven updates reduce the gap between a change and its availability for targeting.

Conceptual view · simplified to explain the change

Built a shared Kafka wrapper adopted across teams for configuration, retries and dead-letter queues. Implemented idempotency, backpressure, queue priorities and autoscaling in event-driven services.

KafkaBrazeEvent-driven architecture
